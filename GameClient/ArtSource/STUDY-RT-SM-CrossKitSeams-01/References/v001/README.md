# STUDY-RT-SM-CrossKitSeams-01 — v001

Cross-material visual study of `KIT-RT` rough timber and `KIT-SM` stone masonry. This is an assembly-study package, not a reusable component or completed construction kit.

The concept sheet depicts repeated/stacked and rotated joins, inside/outside corners, a T, rectangular and semicircular openings, a stone foundation / timber wall transition, a roof/wall example and contact-face overlay. The clean view isolates a simple direct stone-to-timber full-cell contact seam. These remain proposals on the 0.5 m target grid; generated pixels do not verify shape masks or mesh dimensions.

The sheet's corner, roof, opening and seam depictions are visual coverage cues only. Missing window/door interfaces, detailed component variants, curve joins and exact arch geometry remain open in the linked finite matrix. Gameplay material profiles remain separate and unchanged.
