# VFX blueprint: lamps, flames, smoke, fire

Scope: street-lamp and building-lantern FX (`FX_StreetLamp_*`), chimney smoke, and any new flame or fire. Material and texture authoring lives in `GameClient/ArtSource/VFX/StreetLamp/`; placement in `GameClient/ArtSource/Greenlands/lamp_fx.py` and `Silverbrook/silverbrook_props.py`. Lamps: no Niagara, shared HISM cards + material graphs built by Python. Chimneys: one Niagara sprite system (§2); its material and placement are Python, the system asset is authored once in the editor and committed (Niagara stacks are not scriptable in 5.8). Depth: `GameClient/ArtSource/VFX/StreetLamp/README.md`, `docs/evidence/graphics/lamp-fx-3/REVIEW.md`. Shared rules: [README.md](README.md).

Committed values (`lamp-fx.json`): `flame_colour` [1.0,0.38,0.08], `glass_emission_strength` 1.8 (0-4), `light_intensity` 900 lm (0-1200), `light_radius` 4.0 m (0.5-5), `flicker_speed` 1.7 Hz, `flicker_amount` 0.12, `smoke_opacity` 0.09 (0-0.2), `firefly_chance` 0.28 (0-0.6), `firefly_count` 3, `lit` `always`. Cards (`lamp_fx.py apply()`): two crossed additive flame cards 10x22 cm at the socket, two halo cards 26 cm, smoke 16x25 cm at +45 cm, fireflies 1.8 cm crossed-dot cards 1.3-2.6 m away gated by sun elevation; HISM end cull 1800 cm (2500 fireflies). Point light: movable, shadowless, named `unreal.Color(r=255, g=150, b=65, a=255)`. Street-lamp glass components disable Nanite (Nanite does not render translucent glass). Light and flame positions come from each imported mesh's own `*Glow` material pieces (`lamp_fx.glass_sockets`, merged within 60 cm; a flat pane gets its light 10 cm in front; on buildings a glow volume >= 30 cm in every axis or centred under 1 m is a lit room/cellar grille, not a lantern, and gets none): the GLB-derived `lamp-sockets.json` points of building meshes were 2-20 m off their glass (owner: light from nowhere); the JSON now only lists eligible meshes. Each PointLight is attached to its lamp/building actor; wall-mounted lights (owner not `StreetLamp_*`) cast shadows (444 of 795). `dress_all` re-runs `lamp_fx.apply()` last, after `dress_footings` moves houses. Gate: `py -3 tools/Run-InEditor.py GameClient/ArtSource/Greenlands/check_lights.py` -> `LIGHT_CHECK lights 795 on_glass 795 orphans 0 in_wall 0 unshadowed_wall 0 shadowed 444`.

## 2. Chimney smoke — Niagara sprites (spec, not yet passing; WP6g review, `docs/evidence/graphics/2026-10-21-wp6-town-g/REVIEW.md`; WP6h build `docs/evidence/graphics/2026-10-21-wp6-town-h/README.md`)
Static cards failed six reviews (WP6b-g): a fixed rectangle seen through two view-angle terms cannot put the dense end at the pot or grow puffs as they rise. Chimneys are now camera-facing lit sprites from one Niagara system; the lamps keep their cards. The system is built by a committed script that drives the editor's own Niagara stack operations (NiagaraToolsets over the editor MCP), so no editor clicks.

## 3. New flame or fire (recipe)
Copy the lamp flame: two crossed additive cards at the emitter origin (offset 0), texture from `generate_textures.py`, flicker from the two-frequency graph, a warm shadowless point light with named RGB, smoke as one lit `M_LampSmoke` card above the emitter (the lamp wisp, §1; chimney-scale plumes use §2's Niagara system), fireflies only if asked. Sizes scale with the chamber: 16x32 cm flame in a ~30 cm lantern (pass-3 fix), halo 2-3x the glass width. Gate = §1 step 5 offsets + a night capture with the sun below the horizon.

| Flames outside the lantern, "sperm looking things" (ribbons, velocity-aligned ember trails) | flame/halo origins at the socket (0 cm; tolerance 3 cm); no ribbons, no velocity alignment, no ember cards; fireflies are small crossed dots with WPO drift |

| No smoke above the lid; halo not past the cage; mid-street lamps lose glow at 18 m | smoke card at +85 cm, 0.24x0.55 m, `smoke_opacity` 0.18; halo 0.80 m, halo/firefly end cull 4000 cm |
