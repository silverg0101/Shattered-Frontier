Assets `/Game/Frontier/UI/Widgets/{Common,Hud,Unit}/WBP_*` (Unit: `WBP_UnitFrame` and its children `WBP_PlayerFrame`, `WBP_TargetFrame`, `WBP_PartyFrame`, `WBP_RaidFrame`) are seeded and migrated by `tools/ui/create_widgets.py` (also headless: `UnrealEditor-Cmd -run=pythonscript`; it duplicates the owner's retired `WBP_HudVitals` into `WBP_UnitFrame` once, migrates `WBP_Hud` in place, `--force` rebuilds); C++ never overrides Designer fonts, sizes, colours or layout (`FSFUiTextStyle::Apply` fills only unset values) (`Run-InEditor.py`; layouts in editor-only `UI/SFWidgetAuthoring.cpp`, adds UnrealEd/UMGEditor/AssetRegistry to the editor Build.cs) and then owned by the owner in the Designer. `ASFPlayerController::GetHudWidget()` creates `WBP_Hud` (z 8) and feeds the hotbar; `USFE1VitalsHudSubsystem` feeds vitals, `USFQuestPilotSubsystem` feeds the tracker (`PresentQuests`, keeps `QUEST_PILOT_CLIENT_VIEW`), `USFGatheringHud` pushes toasts. Test: `ShatteredFrontier.Hud.Widget` and `.Hud.Level` (the pilot's restored `CharacterLevel` reaches the player badge; a target with no level collapses its numeral) (`Private/Tests/SFHudWidgetTests.cpp`; needs the WBPs) plus `.Hud.Layout.Models`; `.Inventory.Panel.Widget` covers `WBP_Inventory`. The target frame is `WBP_Hud.Target` (`USFHudWidget::PresentTarget`, fed from `ASFPlayerController::GetSelectedCombatTarget` in its tick); `PartyList`/`RaidGrid` + `AddPartyMember`/`ClearParty` wait for a party system. Remaining code-built HUD: prompt bar and its target hit message (`SFHudLayout`), ring and station panel (`SFGatheringHud`), defeated window (`SFE1VitalsHud`).

Gathering HUD: `SFGatheringHud.h/.cpp` paints the world channel ring, pushes skill-gain toasts to `WBP_Hud` and owns `SFStationPanel.h/.cpp`, the v5 station panel shown while aiming at a sawmill/forge/workbench (recipe list, have / need rows, craft time, Craft with the IA_Interact key, the running craft as its queue). Their art is Kit-v5 via `DA_HudKit`, loaded through `Framework/SFHudStyle.h` and `Framework/SFScreenArt.h`; `ShatteredFrontier.Gathering.Hud.Models` covers their text helpers. `SFGatheringPilot` validates the owner request, replicated private preference/deadline and captured typed cooldown. Player inventory (Widget Blueprint): `UI/SFInventoryWidget.h/.cpp` (`USFInventoryWidget` on `USFUiWidget`; data, tabs, tooltip text and the recipe-scroll right click; C++ fills `SlotGrid` with one `WBP_Slot` per backpack slot) is `/Game/Frontier/UI/Widgets/Inventory/WBP_Inventory` (seeded by `tools/ui/create_widgets.py` from `InventoryLayout` in `SFWidgetAuthoring.cpp`; bound names in [menus.md](../blueprints/menus.md) section 0). The old code-built panel is deleted; `ESFInventoryTab`/`FSFInventoryEntry` now live in `SFInventoryWidget.h` (the exchange model uses the tab enum). It is a local-only view; `SFE1PlayerController` binds I to toggle it when the controller owns an enabled `SFGatheringPilot`. It reads `USFGatheringPilotComponent::GetInventoryItems()` (owner-replicated MinimalInventory slots plus the equipped tool) every tick, so harvest, sawmill/forge and reward grants appear without new RPCs. `Private/Tests/SFInventoryPanelTests.cpp` owns `ShatteredFrontier.Inventory.Panel` (`.Rows` merge/sort/format against a fake inventory; `.Accessor` against a real plugin inventory); `tools/Test-GatheringPilot.ps1 -Refining` covers grant regression. The panel reads item definitions directly (category from `UseType`, description, weight, `Icon`): All/Materials/Gear/Consumables tabs, hover tooltip (name, description, category and rarity, weight) and icons; it is Visible so tabs click and tooltips show. `.TabsAndTooltip` and `.DefinitionData` cover filtering and the tooltip model; `tools/Import-PilotItemIcons.py` imports `ArtSource/UI/Icons-v1` to `/Game/Frontier/UI/Icons` and sets each pilot item's `Icon`. No drag/drop, use, drop or durable inventory.

Player vitals HUD: `Characters/SFE1VitalsHud.h/.cpp` holds `FSFE1VitalsModel` (health fraction, defeat time, remaining seconds), the defeated-window widget and a world subsystem that observes the local E1 pawn's `ASFE1Character::CombatHealth()` and pushes the health and stamina bars into `WBP_Hud` (`USFHudVitalsWidget`; full health until combat health first replicates). The v5 defeated window (cause, nearest respawn point from `content/world/zones/greenlands_silverbrook.json`, Kit-v5 respawn scenes, respawn button whose ring fills over `USFCombatPilotSettings::PlayerRespawnDelaySeconds`) plus a local unbound desaturation post-process volume needs no RPCs and no change to combat logic; the server still respawns automatically. The character carries no world-space health label; health shows only in this HUD. UI evidence captures use `tools/Capture-TimedShots.py` (`-ExecCmds=py`, timed in-engine `Shot showui` viewport captures with the UI). `Private/Tests/SFE1VitalsHudTests.cpp` owns `ShatteredFrontier.Characters.VitalsHud`.

Trading UI: `Exchange/SFExchangeModel.*` is the UObject-free view-model layer (`ISFExchangeService`, `FSFTradeViewModel` lock/accept/expiry and unlock-on-edit, `FSFMarketViewModel` filter/sort/buy/list, `SFGold` digit-only gold fields); `Exchange/SFExchangeWidgets.*` are `USFTradeWindow`/`USFMarketBoard` on the HUD kit; `Exchange/SFFakeExchange.h` is the in-memory service behind the `-ExchangeUiDemo` screens (F6 trade, F7 market, `SFE1PlayerController`). Concepts: `docs/Game-Design/concept-art/UI/HUD-v1/hud-trade.png`, `hud-market.png`. `Tests/SFExchangeViewModelTests.cpp` owns `ShatteredFrontier.Exchange.ViewModel`. Not yet wired to the gRPC Exchange client (`Services/`, owned by INT-05).
Trading replication: `Exchange/SFExchangeComponent.*` (`USFExchangeComponent`, server-created per controller when `USFGameplayServicesSettings::bExchangeEnabled`) takes client intents by server RPC (`FSFExchangeIntent`, no identity/escrow/fee from the client), builds the Gameplay.Host request with `SFExchangeRequests` (deterministic operation ids), runs it through the backpack FIFO and replies by client RPC (+wallet, partner `ClientTradeEvent`); `FSFExchangeClientService` adapts it to `ISFExchangeService` (completion callbacks). `tools/Test-ExchangeTrade.ps1` + `Tests/SFExchangeNetworkScenario.cpp` (`-ExchangeScenario`) are the two-client run with the exactly-once DB check. Tests: `ShatteredFrontier.Exchange.{ViewModel,Requests,Intent}`.

Kit v4 pieces and item icons: `tools/Import-HudKit.py` (kit.json, `preprocessed` entries) and `tools/Import-ItemIcons.py` (ItemIcons-v1 manifest). Test: `ShatteredFrontier.Hud.Layout.Models`. Follow-ups: stamina/level badge, minimap content, construction status panel.
