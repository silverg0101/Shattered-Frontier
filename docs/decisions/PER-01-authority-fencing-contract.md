## Logical records and trust ownership

Names below describe required fields, not new RPC names or accepted protobuf definitions. Registered IDs remain exact strings validated by their owning registry. Proposed revisions/generations are positive signed 64-bit integers scoped as stated, with no wrap/reuse; existing `uint64 owner_version` needs an explicit checked mapping/profile before schema acceptance. Digests are 32-byte SHA-256 plus a version/profile identity, subject to B-05; timestamps use Backend's protobuf timestamp convention.

## Admission linearization and permission lifetime

Recommended owner extension: use **action-bound durable admission reservations** within the existing construction prepare/decision lifecycle. Allocate Building's immutable pending action/participant set first; that alone reserves no stock and authorizes no mutation. The original semantic intent/hash exists before grant acquisition and never changes to include later grant receipts. Admission references/digests enter immutable prepared evidence and its decision/completion bindings, not the original semantic digest; no grant hash depends on itself. The fixed set must include each authority that supplies a required durable admission fence, including an authority-only World participation when no terrain changes. Additional session/Character authority participation and its protected-owner interfaces require explicit approval; do not silently reuse a terrain RPC for it.

1. Acquire admission reservations in one release-bound order: session authority, Character ownership, World simulation authority, then Building authorization and owning material/geometry prepares. All references bind the same action/hash/actor/operation. Each authority validates and records its grant in one local transaction serialized with that authority's revocation, ownership or handoff transition. It returns only after durability. Cross-domain grants compose explicit reservations; unrelated snapshots are not equivalent evidence.
2. An invalidation that wins locally prevents that grant. A grant that wins may authorize only this immutable action until its decision resolves; invalidation immediately prevents later grants. Proposed semantics allow already admitted work to reach its original commit/abort even if logout, revocation or handoff follows. They do not permit further live commands or result disclosure to an ended session. Security/Character/World must accept this precise boundary; if policy requires cancellation before decision, owners must supply a serialized cancellation/decision fence before enabling the path. No cache TTL or guessed grace substitutes for that decision.
3. Building never treats a lookup followed by a later local write as atomic admission. It records verified durable grant references and acquires its permission/geometry fence under the owning rows. Inventory separately validates automatic supply and sources under its own locks. Claim transfer, permission revocation, cache destruction and conflicting geometry either win first and invalidate prepare or wait behind the durable fence as Construction requires.
4. Make each grant/prepare idempotent by action/hash and store negative/terminal associations needed to prevent late recreation. Failure partway through admission causes the same Building action to abort, with its immutable decision delivered to every fixed participant; an unused participant records a terminal tombstone. Unknown reply is pending until queried/reconciled. No participant releases an acknowledged grant, reservation or fence on an RPC timeout. Undecided expiry uses Building's existing five-second action deadline; it is not a grant TTL.
5. Perform no interservice call under a local database row lock. Durable records retain protection between calls. Immutable authenticated predecessor admission receipts may be required in the declared acquisition order: World may verify session/Character grants before voting, while still validating and reserving its own authority. No participant waits for a later participant's vote or introduces a dependency cycle. Admission authenticity can be checked before locking, but the durable owning reservation is the protection used later. Release only through authenticated abort or matching completion; applied/committed work always rolls forward. Authority-only participants acknowledge the same decision/completion, never issue a second decision.

Until owners define transactional admission at session/Character authorities and the World Redis-to-durable bridge, **new action admission is disabled**. Existing durable actions may recover through independently authenticated service authority. Neither an old grant nor a construction receipt is a general login, ongoing command capability or permission to return private results.

## Delivery validation, barrier and immutable evidence

Each receiver obtains the logical lookup result directly from authenticated authoritative Building, outside its local lock. Lookup binds incarnation/action/hash, current generation/worker, recipient, exact requested phase/payload digest, fixed set and immutable evidence required by that phase. This is a proposed result of an owner-approved interface, not a forwarded bearer permit, new token format, signing service or claim that `GetConstructionAction` already exposes internal authority.

1. Before first prepare or barrier admission, verify enrolled incarnation, exact fixed recipient/action/hash and lookup identity. Under the receiver's durable action row lock, reject lower generation, different same-generation worker, different hash/participant membership, incompatible phase or payload. First generation may create the record; a higher generation enters only through an authenticated barrier. A barrier on an absent record creates an identity-bound empty record/tombstone. Every phase effect, receipt and outbox update checks the stored incarnation/generation/worker in that same transaction.
2. Building takeover increments generation and binds replacement worker under its action lock; every Building progress/decision/completion transaction conditionally checks both. It sends barriers to every fixed participant, including local geometry and authority-only participation. Each receiver serializes barrier and phase effects on its same action record; acknowledgement returns admitted generation and immutable prepared/applied/completed receipts. A verified higher-generation lookup is not permission to discard those receipts.
3. A lookup can become stale before the receiver commits. If the old phase commits **before** that receiver's replacement barrier, it is admitted in that interval and returned for reconciliation; **after** the barrier it rejects. The barrier, not the earlier remote read, is the receiver's fencing linearization point. A delayed intermediate barrier may likewise win before a later one; Building must use acknowledgements for its own current generation, never a higher/different worker's response. Stronger instantaneous global revocation is not promised.
4. Replacement waits for matching barriers/receipts from the entire fixed set before new decision, completion or additional preparation/reservation. Barrier installation and receipt queries are independent of admission-vote ordering and cannot wait for another participant's vote; any remaining new grants/prepares then follow the original acyclic acquisition order, reusing verified immutable predecessor receipts. A committed old-generation effect is reconciled, not repeated or refunded. A newer takeover invalidates the replacement's conditional Building writes. Unavailable authority/participant or ambiguous DB reply leaves recovery pending. A stale worker cannot publish a new decision after takeover; only a previously durable immutable decision can still be transported.
5. Applying commit requires original decision evidence and the receiver's exact prepared digest; completion additionally requires original all-applied certificate and exact local applied receipt. Current delivery authority is validated separately. Historical decision generation need not equal delivery generation. Abort cannot replace commit; a barrier alone cannot release holds or activate geometry. Partial activation keeps existing read/occupancy gates until coherent completed projection is safe.

## Restore enrollment

Stop admission for the named recovery scope; fence the old database primary **and** old workload credentials/network delivery before enrolling a replacement. Record proof outside the restored snapshot's trust boundary through the approved operations authority, then persist the new enrolled incarnation at Building and every affected participant. Enrollment is an explicit transition from the previously admitted identity, never an ordering comparison between opaque values. Unreachable/unfenced domains remain closed; a restored numeric generation is never evidence that fencing succeeded.

Reconcile the eligible decision frontier and participant receipts before any participant accepts normal phases in the new incarnation. Preserve original action/hash, decision/completion identities and origin incarnation; enrollment explicitly authorizes the new incarnation to recover those named records without rewriting them. A receipt contradicting the restored decision frontier blocks reopening for audited reconciliation, never an automatic abort/recharge. Partial enrollment admits no new decisions or activation. Only after all scoped domains acknowledge enrollment and reconcile may ordinary generation barriers/progress resume. Exact recovery authority roles, fencing proof, frontier eligibility and enrollment storage/disaster survival remain B-04/PER-04 owner deliverables.

## Bounded acceptance matrix and remaining decisions

Fixtures below are design requirements, not executed tests. Every case asserts one stable action/hash, conserved materials, immutable decision/receipts and no usable incomplete output or mixed geometry.

| Race / failure | Required observable outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Forged actor/realm; zone sends gateway headers; wrong `srvi_`/allocation/character owner | Reject before grants/material reservations; no identity inferred from body or cached registration. |
| Session revocation / ownership change / handoff vs admission grant | Invalidation first denies the grant; grant first permits only the named action under accepted lifetime policy. No new grants from stale presence; no TTL-derived revocation claim. |
| Claim revocation / cache packing / destruction vs prepare | Winning revision rejects stale prepare, or the durable authorization/source fence serializes the competing change; no later check substitutes for the reservation. |
| World admission requires earlier session/Character grants, including after takeover | Verify exact immutable predecessor grants and reserve current World authority before its own vote; never wait for later Building/Inventory votes. Barrier/query recovery proceeds independently, then missing grants resume in declared order. |
| Lost grant/prepare response; partial admission; delayed prepare after abort | Query original action; persist/replay original abort; terminal tombstone prevents recreation. No automatic grant/hold expiry. |
| Absent participant + delayed old prepare; forged higher generation or different same-generation worker | Barrier tombstone rejects old call; unauthenticated generation/worker changes reject without effect. |
| Old apply just before / after receiver barrier | Before: exact committed receipt reconciled once. After: generation rejection; replacement cannot complete until all current barriers match. |
| Old worker decision / completion write after Building takeover; takeover during barrier delivery | Conditional write rejects; replacement yields to newer generation. Previously persisted decision remains recoverable. |
| Delayed original completion under newer delivery generation; lost completion reply | Same immutable certificate releases only matching applied outputs once; no historical generation rewrite. |
| Missing authority/participant, generation overflow, contradictory restored receipts | Pending/closed; no guessed vote, counter wrap, refund or visible success. |
| Old primary/workload survives restore; delayed old-incarnation packet; partial enrollment | Enrollment/admission blocks until fencing proof and complete reconciliation; cross-incarnation packet rejects even with a larger generation. |

Owners must resolve: **B-03** gateway-only caller/metadata conflict, existing identity mapping and session/Character/World admission APIs, durable serialization and pre-revocation grant lifetime; **B-04** lookup/barrier wire/storage profile, worker identity and restore enrollment/fencing; **B-05** exact typed encodings and digest vectors. These add proposed authority participation and World durability requirements; protected Security/Character/Itemization interfaces need their owners' release before edits. Record decisions against these artifact revisions with scope/closure evidence as PER-01-B requires. Independent review and document checks do not close any gate or establish runtime behavior.
