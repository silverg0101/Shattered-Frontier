# Owner decisions, 2026-10-22

Recorded from the owner's answers in the Task Runner session. Each line is binding scope for the matching register row in `docs/design-work-plan.md`.

| ID | Decision | Owner's words / notes |
|---|---|---|
| DEC-02 | **Accepted.** Construction is durable: structures save to the player's claim, and placement costs real inventory. | Add an **Alpha Starter Kit**: every new character spawns with enough materials to test building and construction. It is a development/alpha grant, declared as typed configuration (contents, quantities, one grant per character), and is not a shipped player feature. |
| DEC-06 | **Accepted: restricted fixed-price market.** Basic goods only (raw materials and simple crafted goods: logs, ore, ingots, planks), seller-set fixed price, sold in a settlement market such as Silverbrook. | Gear and rare items come later. |
| Crafting depth | **Learn recipes + quality.** Recipes are unlocked (trainers, books, practice). Crafted items have quality grades from skill and materials. | |
| Quests | **Build the durable quest system next.** NPCs offer quests through the dialogue board, quest state persists, and rewards land in inventory. | Add a **Quest Board**: a notice board in each city's centre, where that city's questlines start. Quests can be posted there as well as given by NPCs. Meeting the NPC and taking the quest directly stays possible. |
| DEC-07 | **Keep binary assets in plain git for now.** No LFS and no external store. | Revisit if clone or worktree size becomes a blocker. |
| DEC-05 | **Target mid-range to high-end PCs**, with an in-game graphics menu: Low / Medium / High / Epic / Ultra presets that act as templates, plus individual settings players can fine-tune, "like every other game". | Reference: about RTX 3060-class at 1080p High, 60 fps as the mid-range bar; Ultra for high-end. |
| DEC-09 | **Outside testers after the core loop works**: gathering, building, crafting, market and quests saving and working together. A small invited group then joins on the steam-server. | Hosting cost and tester-data handling still need owner approval at that point. |
| DEC-03 scope | **Don't limit digging without a valid reason.** Valid limits: no falling through the world (bedrock floor), no digging through region boundaries, and no digging in safe areas or cities. Also off limits are roads, spawns and other players' claims, which are the existing protections. Public edits outside a claim heal after **7 days**, as the design already says. | The owner also asked whether the planned Landscape terrain (GFX-02) affects this. It does: a heightfield Landscape can't be dug into at runtime. The TER-01 spike (UE 5.8 Mesh Terrain) runs **before GFX-02 J1**, and a terrain decision follows: Mesh Terrain everywhere, Landscape plus dig zones, or a hybrid. |
| Terrain choice rule | **If TER-01 shows a clean dig-anywhere solution, adopt it** with the limits above, and don't ask again. **If digging needs designated areas, don't build digging at all.** No dig zones get designed. Players get better building foundations instead (foundations that adapt to uneven ground), and the ground stays the photoreal GFX-02 terrain. | Owner, 2026-10-22: "if we cant and there has to be designated areas, i would rather not design that. Players can just build foundations better." |
| QST-02 owner questions | Players can drop a quest at any time and take it again from the start later. **No limit** on quests carried. The Quest Board shows **every quest in town**, with locked ones greyed and the reason shown. | Answers to the QST-02 "Owner questions". |
| CRF-01 owner questions | Crafts **never fail**: a crafter below the skill gets a Crude result. Unknown recipes are **shown greyed, with where to learn them**. Trainers charge a **small fee that rises with difficulty** (about 120 copper for the first). | |
| MKT-01 owner questions | The Alpha Starter Kit includes a **purse of about 5 silver (500 copper)**. **Each town has its own market**, so goods are carried between towns. **Fees are kept** in alpha: 1% to list, 5% on sale. | |
| Digging limits | Digging gets limits that block grief holes but allow building. Digging into a mountainside for a house is fine **if the player claims that area**. Cutting a small ridge or slope down to make room for a house is allowed. **Digging straight down to make grief holes is not.** | Owner, 2026-10-22. Fable is proposing the concrete rules. |
| TER-02 owner questions | Open-land depth **2 m**. Levelling a house pad on open land needs **no claim** (it heals in 7 days unless claimed). Buffer from roads, paths and others' land **3 m**. Unhealed open-land digging per player **1,000 m³** (about 23 m × 23 m × 2 m). In your own claim, dig to **8 m** below the claim anchor. Trapped visitors use an **Unstuck** command. | Answers to TER-02's "Owner questions". |
| Terrain tools in building mode | Digging lives **inside the building menu as a separate action**, alongside placing pieces rather than as a held tool. **Raising ground** is a matching action under **the same limits**: 2 m above the original ground on open land, the claim band inside a claim, the same buffers, the same per-player volume budget and 7-day healing. | Owner, 2026-10-22. It shares the build grid and preview colours (CLM-01). |
| Terrain tool preview + sizes | Dig and Raise show a **visual preview of exactly how the ground will change** before you commit: the ghost of the resulting surface, how much is removed or added, and where the dig gets clipped or blocked. They also come in **different tool sizes**, small to large brushes the player picks in the Terrain section. | Owner, 2026-10-22. |
| Healing rules | Healing must be defined fully. **Healing never fills over anything that occupies the space**: a player, a placed structure or block, or a dropped or placed item. Those voxels and their surroundings are left alone. | Owner, 2026-10-22. The full rules go in TER-02. |
| DEC-04 / DEC-08 | **Pending.** Plain-language summaries were given on 2026-10-22, and the owner's OK is awaited. | |
