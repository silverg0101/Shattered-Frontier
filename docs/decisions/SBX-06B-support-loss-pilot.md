# SBX-06B: standalone support-loss implementation packet

Applies only to the isolated sandbox and Construction §3.4 support algorithm v1. The prior fixed-foundation checkpoint temporarily rejects destabilizing removal; this assignment replaces that guard with the designed confirmation/grace/collapse flow. No Inventory, salvage-item grant, damage RPC, terrain editing, real-ground or production recovery integration is authorized by this packet.

## Authority and confirmation

Preview the candidate removal with the same solver and report affected unsupported addresses/count. If additional members become unsupported, require an explicit confirmation intent bound to the same cell and expected assembly revision. Authority repeats ownership, mode, reach, LOS, geometry and support checks at confirmation. Any accepted edit or collapse makes the old preview stale; do not silently refresh and execute a previous confirmation. This is the acting builder's choice, not a new property-owner veto. Keep current keys temporary: a clearly prompted Enter may confirm a recorded removal, with build toggle/selection change/cancel clearing it.

When a confirmed removal commits, start each newly unsupported surviving member's deadline from authoritative world time. Default grace is 10 seconds. Store one deadline per actual member; repeated previews, retries, unrelated edits or continued instability do not extend it. Recovery cancels the deadline; a subsequent distinct support-loss episode starts a new one. Validate finite nondecreasing clock inputs in the pure lifecycle helper. Production code supplies the local world's clock, never client time.

During grace, a new member must itself be supported and may not destabilize any previously supported survivor. Already unstable members with active deadlines may remain unstable while the builder adds a valid column in several steps; their existing deadlines stay unchanged. This is the bounded clarification of valid new placement during recovery, not permission to create floating members. Always evaluate the full candidate; exemptions name only already unstable members from the current authority snapshot. Missing physical projections or unavailable foundation proof fence edits.

## Collapse and safe activation

At a due deadline, recompute actual support. Recovered members survive. Remove only due members that remain unsupported, through the same staged multi-member projection transaction as ordinary edits; publish no partial member/revision/collision change on staging failure. Expired deadlines remain due on a failed rebuild. On success, advance the assembly revision, destroy removed projections, refresh neighbor surfaces and recompute support for survivors. Preserve independently supported sections. Any newly unsupported survivor starts its own episode; process later waves explicitly rather than blindly deleting a connected assembly.

No persistent rubble collision is created. Development collapse logs/counts must explicitly say economic salvage is disabled; do not mint test inventory or replace the owning 25% production salvage entitlement. The future paid system still owes its contribution/provenance rules. Ordinary voluntary demolition/reclaim percentages also remain intact outside this unlimited-stock fixture.

The synthetic foundation is fixed and identified by the prior helper. Unexpected missing/changed proof makes support unavailable and fences edits/collapse until repaired; it is not an authorized terrain-removal experiment. Re-evaluate support after edits/collapse; with no damage/terrain mutation route, no new arbitrary periodic world scan is required. Tick only needs to check pending deadlines and perform a due-wave evaluation.

Grace is a declared typed release-only setting with shipped 10 s default, safe 1–60 s bounds, seconds units, reviewed release-author permission and next sandbox-start application. Freeze the state snapshot; no CDO mutation, live console or client override. Algorithms, material profile and topology stay in versioned code/content.

HUD/ghost feedback must distinguish stable, strained and unstable, show remaining grace and pending confirmation, and explain why removal/placement is blocked. Do not claim full limiting-contact visualization unless it is actually delivered. Clearing an input selection must not auto-submit an old placement/removal intent. Inherited movement/E stays unchanged; no construction route in normal E1 or network modes.
