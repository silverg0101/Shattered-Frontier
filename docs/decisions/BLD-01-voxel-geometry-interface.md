# BLD-01 — Structural voxel geometry interface v1

Status: design baseline selected under the user's 2026-10-18 instruction to define and lock the interface before final meshes. Independent review is recorded in the work plan. This locks the architecture and asset contract, not unbuilt content or runtime readiness. No final structural mesh is accepted until its per-shape numerical fixtures and engine checks below pass. Document-local field names are not registered wire schemas.

## Authority and scope

[Construction §§3.1–3.5, 8, 15–16](../Game-Design/Legends_Shattered_Frontier_Construction_System_Architecture_v0.1.md) owns placement, materials, support, health, budgets and functional definitions. [Construction Integration §§3–4, 8](../Game-Design/Legends_Shattered_Frontier_Construction_Integration_Contracts_v0.1.md) owns addressed assembly members, durable decisions and provenance. [DEC-03](DEC-03-terrain.md) owns the separate terrain-adoption boundary. [Tuning](../Game-Design/Legends_Shattered_Frontier_Tuning_and_Configuration_Architecture_v0.1.md) makes geometry, algorithms and topology release-bound. This decision refines those interfaces without replacing support, damage, transaction or permission rules.

Selected: **sparse, addressed structural cells with versioned partial solids; deterministic exposed-surface generation with material-kit treatments**. Foundations/walls/roofs are brushes and recipes over cells. They are not indivisible wall prefabs, and structural solids are not written into World's smooth terrain density. Functional objects remain separately defined entities with their own ports, access and collision envelopes.

Terrain may use its eventual smooth density mesher. Structural geometry uses authored polygonal solids and exact shared contact boundaries. The Unreal rendering/cooking implementation remains selectable behind this interface; no plugin or engine API is selected here. Alternatives rejected for this baseline: full-cube-only occupancy loses partial shapes; smooth density for structures blurs openings/joints and changes support inputs; fixed wall prefabs prevent the planned cell-level editing and destruction.

## Coordinate and orientation contract

- A cell is exactly 0.5 m; address is `(zone_id, ix, iy, iz)`, signed integer coordinates in the zone-local frame. The zone origin must be supplied by the owning World coordinate mapping, never inferred from a render actor. X/Y are horizontal, Z is up.
- Cell-local geometry uses integer coordinates `0..65536` inclusive on each axis, with one unit = `0.5/65536 m`. Minimum corner is the pivot. This is structural geometry precision, not terrain Q15 density or a finer player placement grid.
- A cell owns the half-open address volume `[i,i+1)` for indexing. Its closed solid includes contact boundaries; two cells sharing a plane do not double-count volume. Negative addresses use mathematical floor division. A 16 m spatial bucket has 32 cells/axis; local address is `i - 32*floor(i/32)`. Spatial bucketing reuses World mapping, not a new chunk-key packing or terrain storage owner.
- There are 24 proper cube rotations (signed axis permutations with determinant +1), identified by the ordered images of local +X,+Y,+Z. Each shape explicitly allowlists its subset, reached in 90-degree steps. Identity only is valid unless more are authored. Reflections require a separately authored shape; no negative scale, arbitrary rotation or non-unit structural scale.
- Rotation is affine, not multiplication about the minimum corner: for `Q=65536`, local point `v` becomes `R*(v-(Q/2,Q/2,Q/2))+(Q/2,Q/2,Q/2)`. For a brush, first transform its already cell-oriented points `p=Q*offset+v` by `R*p`, then subtract the minimum corner `m` of the rotated **declared full grid envelope**. Add the destination grid anchor after this normalization. Split transformed points into their member cell and normalized local geometry using the transformed source cell envelope, not vertex-flooring at shared boundary planes. Thus a negative output axis maps source index `i` to `B-1-i` for source envelope extent `B`, and local coordinate `v` to `Q-v`; a positive axis maps them to `i` and `v`. Compose brush and member rotations, checking the resulting shape rotation allowlist. Persist the normalized offsets/rotations; empty brush margins cannot shift the anchor.
- Blender/Unreal adapters must declare axis/handedness and unit conversion (metres to centimetres = ×100), prove positive orientation after conversion, and preserve the minimum-corner pivot. Functional hinges have additional named local pivots.

## Immutable content and live member records

| Record | Required data / rule |
| --- | --- |
| Shape definition | Stable local content key + version + SHA-256 of canonical compiled payload; nonempty solid inside one cell; closed outward-oriented boundary; deterministic convex decomposition; six boundary contact regions; occupied volume fraction; allowed rotations. No self-intersection, non-manifold boundary, zero-volume solid or disconnected interior. Nonconvex but connected shapes use several convex parts with disjoint interiors. |
| Geometry brush | Versioned list of distinct integer cell offsets, each with one shape/version and rotation; anchor at minimum grid corner of brush envelope; declared bounds and empty opening regions. Brush identity is authoring/placement provenance, not a second damage or support entity. |
| Structural member | Address, shape/version, rotation, material-profile/version, cosmetic-style/version, assembly ID, member generation/revision, combat health/condition and contribution/repair references. One nonempty solid record per address; absence means empty. Member generation changes on geometry replacement; existing ledger rules govern repair/material changes. |
| Material kit | Versioned face, corner, joint, opening and transition treatments referencing the same canonical geometry. Gameplay material and cosmetic identity stay separate. |
| Functional attachment | Versioned definition, transform, support anchors, ports, motion and access volumes. A door is an object, not a hole-filling structural skin. |

Build-time compilation converts the art contract's analytic reference shapes into canonical cell solids. Vertices are integer lattice coordinates; planar faces and convex parts are explicit. Round-to-nearest, ties-to-even is used only during offline quantization; runtime never re-tessellates curves from floating-point formulas. The compiled payload, not an image or platform trigonometry result, defines occupancy. The compiler must record analytic-to-compiled error, volume and opening-clearance comparisons; the compiler cannot quietly shrink a required passage. Curved surfaces are therefore a versioned polygonal approximation, not a claim of mathematically exact circular physics.

Contact regions are exact intersections of that solid with each cell plane, including holes. Occupied fraction is the exact rational union volume divided by the full cell volume; convex interiors must not overlap. Derive contact polygons and fraction from the same compiled solid, verify any stored accelerators match, and use exact integer/rational predicates for interface tests. Never estimate mass/contact from render triangles, texture alpha or a full-cell occupancy bit. Existing gameplay formulas consume these fractions/areas with their own numeric rules; this geometry decision does not redefine terrain or economy rounding.

Canonical payloads are UTF-8 JSON without BOM/whitespace: fixed field order `interface_version`, `shape_key`, `shape_version`, `vertices`, `parts`, `rotations`; integers in decimal, no floats. Keys use ASCII `[a-z0-9._-]+`; versions are positive integers. Vertices sort lexicographically and are unique; parts contain outward planar polygon faces referencing that vertex table. Each face cycle starts at its least vertex index, preserves winding; faces and parts sort lexicographically by their index sequences; rotations sort by their signed axis tuples. Reject duplicate/degenerate faces, redundant duplicate parts, unknown fields or noncanonical payloads. The SHA-256 addresses these exact bytes. Derived contact/volume reports have their own hashes referencing this payload. A golden file and validator must prove canonicalization before any compiled shape is approved. This is a content-file contract, not an invented RPC codec or a Registry identifier allocation.

## Placement brushes, edits and damage

The ten [art reference shapes](../art-templates/player-construction-kit-contract.md) remain the initial authoring targets. Full/half/quarter/post/slope/corners/stair are single-cell targets. The 2 m arch and quarter-annulus are **multi-cell brushes**, clipped at every 0.5 m cell plane into reusable versioned fragment shapes. Record their brush-to-fragment table and summed volume. No bounding-box fill is permitted. Cells wholly inside openings produce no member and no structural cost/budget use. Where clipping would produce a disconnected cell interior, reject that brush compilation and revise/split the authoring target explicitly; do not connect it invisibly or create multiple independently supported solids in one member.

Placement expands and rotates the brush first, validates every actual member and relevant clearance, then uses the existing paid action. The 256-cell structural commit ceiling counts every changed nonempty address, not one brush/arch. The initial reference brushes must fit one batch; larger recipes split into explicitly previewed independent paid batches with valid intermediate support. Blueprints inherit existing cell/object limits. Brush rotations rotate cell offsets and local solids together; do not rotate each fragment around the brush origin incorrectly.

V1 admits one shape per address. Complementary partial shapes are not independently stacked inside one address. Replacing an occupied address is an explicit before/after transaction with the normal permission, collision, support, payment/refund and generation rules; never silently union shapes, charge twice, inherit destroyed health or mint a second salvage basis. Material paint retains geometry and follows the existing material-upgrade rules. Shape replacement is not the material paint operation.

Damage, condition, support weight/capacity and refund basis belong to each actual member, not the brush or visual mesh. A hit must intersect that member's solid; holes cannot receive structural hits. Damage does not carve arbitrary new sub-cell holes: intact damaged shapes keep their solid until a member is removed or explicitly replaced by another approved shape. Existing health/support/collapse and salvage rules apply. Removing one arch fragment removes that member only; other fragments survive only if supported. Assembly splits preserve cell provenance, health, repair accrual and contribution allocation under Integration §8; regrouping never duplicates those records. Empty space in a brush claims no build permission or support, but all applicable functional/traversal clearance checks still apply.

## Surface, contact and seam interface

For each occupied cell, transform its canonical solid, fetch the six face neighbors and calculate the shared-plane intersection of their contact regions. Cull only the overlapping region; retain uncovered partial faces. Boundary edges/points have zero support area. Contact between partial solids uses actual positive-area polygon overlap, scaled under Construction §3.4; appearance or bounding-box adjacency cannot add support. Within-cell convex decomposition faces are internal and removed. Structural support requires continuous solid within a member, as enforced at compile time.

Rendered exposed surfaces are partitioned into canonical surface patches. A patch records source member/generation, local face or curved facet, orientation, material/style and boundary contour. Treatment selection is a pure versioned rule over those patches, actual adjacent geometry/materials and world-grid anchors. Never choose joints from whether a neighboring mesh happened to stream in. Faces at an unloaded neighbor are unresolved, not authoritative empty; obtain a revisioned border snapshot before publishing the affected seam.

- Material-kit face/corner/jamb/joint assets are surface treatments, not replacement occupancy. Apply them only on compatible named patch/interface roles. New silhouette, support or access geometry requires a different shape/attachment definition.
- V1 boundary vertices and clear-opening contours stay fixed. Decorative geometry stays inside the solid's canonical envelope and does not bridge empty portions or create collision/support. Normal/parallax/color detail cannot make a hole solid. Structural damage/condition cues remain authoritative and material tiers readable.
- Material coordinates derive from zone-local grid position, declared axis direction and versioned kit repeat scale/phase. They do not restart per render chunk. Curves carry authored compatible patch coordinates. Assembly-level overrides must explicitly preserve seam phase; random per-cell orientation cannot break grain/course matching.
- A mixed-material contact has no duplicate coplanar internal face. At exposed material junctions, transition assets name both kits; stable ordering by material/style keys selects one owner. If no authored transition exists, use the documented neutral butt seam and mark missing art coverage; never invent a stronger material or alter contact planes.
- Damage/removal exposes the surviving neighbor's real cut face with its material, not a prepainted hole or missing underside. Kits must supply these faces. Render LOD may reduce interior visual detail but cannot move contact/opening silhouettes, change gameplay collision or determine gameplay occlusion/shelter.

Meshing jobs consume a revisioned member snapshot plus its face-neighbor border; material treatments may declare a finite larger read-only halo. Invalidation expands by that declared halo, including across spatial buckets. Sort inputs by address, shape hash and rotation; any variant selection uses a versioned deterministic key from those inputs. A result names all input revisions and content/algorithm hashes; discard it if any dependency changed. Chunk size, LOD and instancing are rendering choices, never durable member identity. Adjacent visible chunks must use the same boundary snapshot or remain behind the activation barrier until coherent.

## Gameplay projection and safe activation

Collision is derived from the same canonical solid (for example its validated convex decomposition), with no full bounding-box substitute across a hole. Render facets are not authority. Support uses contact regions; shelter/weather/smoke queries use occupied surfaces and empty apertures according to their existing algorithms. Navigation and functional access use canonical clearance volumes plus collision; neither an attractive arch nor 2 m image annotation certifies traversal. Cosmetic changes do not rebuild gameplay topology.

An edit's validation read set includes touched members, support/anchor dependencies, neighboring contacts, functional/motion/access volumes and authoritative terrain intersection/anchor inputs. Bind their revisions and content versions to the existing quote and durable action; revalidate/fence them under Integration. Building persists structures; World persists terrain. Their solids remain distinct, with union collision/queries and explicit terrain-anchor checks. A material texture cannot compact fill or create ground anchoring.

Prepare collision/support/occupancy for the candidate generation before activation. Bind mesh/collision jobs to that generation and publish a coherent set; asynchronous callbacks cannot install an old result. A committed action awaiting collision recovery stays held/unavailable under the existing completion protocol, never reports usable success or receives a timeout refund. Fence the changed collision/access footprint so characters cannot enter newly occupied geometry or use stale walkable surfaces. Clients lacking required content/snapshots cannot interact with that footprint; old visuals cannot grant placement authority. Restart reconstructs members/versions and rebuilds projections before releasing that fence.

## Acceptance and next assignments

This v1 decision locks address/solid identity, partial-cell behavior, brush decomposition, allowed transforms, immutable content and cosmetic separation. Numeric content changes, compiler/canonicalization changes or incompatible boundary rules require a new interface/content version, explicit migration/replay review and compatible client/server release. No live console expression or operator voxel-resolution switch is allowed. New references are local content keys until Shared Registry integration explicitly allocates canonical identifiers.
