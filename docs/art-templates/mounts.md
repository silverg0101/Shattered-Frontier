# Mount concept template

Copy the prompt, replace only MAIN CONCEPT, and follow the [library workflow](README.md). Attached references are optional.

This prompt defaults to a concept sheet. For the separate clean asset image used later by 3D generation, apply [the shared asset-view override](generate-images.md#clean-asset-view-prompt-block); keep rider and scale guides in the sheet only.

```text
MAIN CONCEPT: [Describe the mount in one sentence.]

Create one cohesive mount concept for Shattered Frontier, suitable
as a visual reference for Blender modeling, materials and rigging.
Develop unspecified details from the concept. Concept requirements
override defaults; keep one coherent design across every view.

DESIGN DIRECTION
Use grounded high fantasy with a practical frontier character.
Favor a strong silhouette, believable anatomy, natural materials,
and equipment that looks constructed and usable.
This is a working style default. Follow attached project artwork
for palette, materials and detail density when supplied.
Borrow appearance cues only; do not copy its typography, logos,
branding or collage layout. No image attachment is required.

SCALE AND RIDER
Design for one adult humanoid rider approximately 1.8 meters tall.
Choose a plausible mount size and show a simple rider silhouette
in the perspective view, with a clearly separated scale marker.
Make mounting, seating, steering and dismounting believable.
Allow clearance for the rider's legs, equipment and locomotion.

ANATOMY
Choose anatomy and locomotion appropriate to the main concept.
Keep limb count, joints, proportions and weight distribution
consistent across all views. Show how it carries its own weight
and the rider. Make muscle masses and articulation readable.
Separate limbs enough to understand their construction.
For magical anatomy, retain a consistent modelable structure.

SADDLE AND EQUIPMENT
Include fitted riding equipment appropriate to the creature:
a saddle or equivalent seat, padding, secure harness, handholds
or reins, and foot support where the anatomy permits.
Show complete attachment paths that avoid breathing surfaces,
joints and wing movement. No straps passing through anatomy.
Keep tack removable and distinct from the creature's body.
Use restrained leather, woven fabric, wood and functional metal
where appropriate. Heavy armor appears only if the concept calls
for it; do not add weapons or unrelated cargo by default.

REFERENCE SHEET
Show the SAME mount in these separated panels:
1. A large three-quarter view with the rider scale silhouette.
2. Front, side and rear orthographic views without the rider.
3. A smaller untacked side view revealing the underlying anatomy.
4. Close-ups of head, saddle attachment and distinctive feet,
   claws, hooves, wings or other important structures.
If wings hide anatomy, add one clearly labeled extended-wing
detail using the same design rather than another creature.
Use a neutral standing/resting pose appropriate to its anatomy.
Align orthographic views at matching scale. Preserve tail,
limbs, markings and equipment placement between panels.
Use a plain neutral background, soft even light and minimal shadow.

MATERIALS AND MODELING
Distinguish skin, fur, scales, feathers, horn and tack materials
where present. Describe forms through shape rather than painted
shading. Prioritize silhouette, then medium forms, then fine detail.
Keep articulation and attachment points legible for an animated
game asset. Do not depict invented wireframes or UV diagrams.

AVOID
Extra/fused limbs, impossible joints, floating equipment,
intersecting straps, obstructed silhouettes, dramatic perspective
in orthographic panels, ornate backgrounds and inconsistent views.

OUTPUT
One polished modeling reference sheet for one mount design.
Prioritize anatomy, rider fit, construction and material clarity.
```

The sheet is a visual guide, not a mesh or dimensionally exact drawing. Verify rider fit, scale and anatomy in Blender before approving the asset.
