Work order: complete `KIT-RT` and `KIT-SM`, then retain `KIT-WT`, `KIT-RM` and `KIT-MFM` as explicitly queued complete kits, not declared complete or optional.

For every role below, instantiate one separately indexed component package for each applicable kit using stable IDs `BLD-<KIT>-<role>-01`. Role IDs are listed in the matrix; role names are fixed once queued. `geometry_ref` stays proposal v1 until the construction content owner accepts it.
