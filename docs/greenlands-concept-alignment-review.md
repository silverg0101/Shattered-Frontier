# Greenlands concept alignment review

2026-10-19. User-requested visual review. **The current preview is a useful environment block-in with detailed local assets; it does not yet match the Greenlands concept’s look and feel.** This is a visual judgment from the images below, separate from traversal/gameplay checks.

## References and current evidence

**2026-10-19 update:** [Greenlands-Region-v2](Game-Design/concept-art/Greenlands-Region-v2.webp) now supersedes Designer (6) for the region map, settlement names and locations; see the [Greenlands region definition](Game-Design/Regions/Legends_Shattered_Frontier_Region_Greenlands_v0.1.md). Designer (6) remains a mood and palette reference for this review.

Primary target: [Designer (6) — The Greenlands](Game-Design/concept-art/Designer%20(6).png). Its intended mood is lush, bright, welcoming and fertile, with rolling valleys, clear water, mixed woodland and friendly settlements. [Concept](Game-Design/concept-art/Concept.png) and [Designer (9)](Game-Design/concept-art/Designer%20(9).png) support the same regional identity. City/stronghold/siege concepts establish broader world ambition; they do not require building a capital city into this starter preview. Concept-map labels are visual/design context, not a new approved world layout.

Compared actual current images in `GameClient/ArtSource/GreenlandsPreview/`: `Settlement-Finished-InGame.png` and `Cottage-Finished-Front-InGame.png` (01:59 local), `Arrival-Woodland-Detail-InGame.png`, `Arrival-Meadow-InGame.png`, `Settlement-Meadow-InGame.png` and `Wildflower-Patch-InGame.png`. The broad arrival/settlement views matter more to this comparison than isolated close-ups. The separate grey construction/harvesting sandbox is a mechanics fixture and cannot represent finished Greenlands art.

| Aspect | Concept target | Observed gap / correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landscape composition | Layered foreground, inhabited valley, winding routes and distant mountain/woodland silhouettes | Current horizon is largely empty pale terrain; views feel flat and disconnected. Compose a representative arrival vista with foreground framing, rolling middle distance and a readable destination. |
| Palette and light | Healthy varied greens, warm sun, blue sky/water, readable cool shade | Dark olive/brown drooping conifers and pale ground dominate current views. Preserve contrast and material detail while bringing back healthy greens and welcoming light; brightness alone is insufficient. |
| Woodland | Broadleaf canopy, mixed groves, mature trunks and layered undergrowth | Conifer walls dominate the arrival. Use broadleaf silhouettes and layered edges in focal areas; keep conifers as part of a coherent mix. More repeated trees will not solve species/silhouette imbalance. |
| Settlement | Finished timber/plaster/stone homes, credible pitched roofs, maintained yards and human-scale gathering places | Current cottages still read as exposed framing/patched panels, with heavy flattened thatch forms and cold glazing. Finish coherent building envelopes, roof silhouettes and entrances before adding prop quantity. |
| Meadows and paths | Rich grass cover, flower accents, open usable ground and natural transitions | Close-up vegetation has detail, but repeated spiky clumps over pale ground leave a sparse/patchy distant read. Blend grass height, ground color and irregular planting drifts; distinguish maintained yards from wild edges. |
| Regional identity | Clear rivers/lakes, stone crossings, fertile valleys, welcoming landmark | Reviewed views do not yet communicate water/crossing or a strong Greenlands landmark. Incorporate a suitable planned water/bridge or landscape cue into the arrival composition; giant castles/waterfalls are not required to establish the mood. |

## Next visual pass

The environment chat owns its map/art changes. First finish coherent cottage walls/roofs and one representative arrival-to-settlement composition within the existing connected layout. Then address woodland mix, meadow transitions and regional landmark/water cues in that same view. Keep construction plots and traversal routes usable. Harvest resources must use the same material scale/palette and believable grounded silhouettes; an available source is not accepted merely because it is free.

Re-capture the same player-height arrival, settlement and woodland views after meaningful changes. Compare those directly with the primary concept for composition, mood, silhouette and material coherence. A concept-aligned result still needs later integrated gameplay/performance evidence; existing walk/clearance passes do not establish art acceptance.
