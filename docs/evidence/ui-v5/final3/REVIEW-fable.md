# UI v5 final3 — independent acceptance review

Reviewer: Fable (art director / acceptance judge), 2026-10-22. Inputs: the nine concepts in `docs/Game-Design/concept-art/UI/v5/`, the nine `final3/*-live.png` frames (read at full 1920x1080 and as 2x side-by-side crops), `final3/pixel/<screen>/report.md`, the `*-live.json` widget rects, `elements/<screen>.json`, `docs/blueprints/menus.md` 1-2, `docs/visual-review.md`, `final2/REVIEW-fable.md` (context only), `Kit-v5/kit.json` + PNGs, and the widget code named below. All px are 1080p design px; dx/dy/dw/dh are live minus concept from the pixel report; cap heights were measured on the frames (threshold scan of one glyph).

Result: **0 PASS / 9 FAIL.** Every screen fails on geometry, type and colour; four frames (gameplay, gathering, crafting-station, loading) are also not captured in the concept's state.

## Cross-screen root causes (each per-screen list refers to these)

- **A. Every text renders 4/3 too large.** `SFHudStyle::RoleFont` (`Public/Framework/SFHudStyle.h:228-231`), `DisplayHeading` (`:281`) and `LoadingScreenFont` (`Private/Framework/SFLoadingScreen.cpp:72`) pass the design px straight into `FSlateFontInfo::Size`, which is points at 96 DPI (1 pt = 1.333 px). Measured: `Button(…)` caption "24" → "Cancel Trade" cap 21 px (EB Garamond cap ≈ 0.66 em → 32 px em), concept cap 17 px (26 px em); tracker title "24 bold" cap 21 vs concept 21 regular at 28 px; "Defeated" 72 → cap 64 (96 px em), concept cap 53 (76-80 px em). This is the dialogue reviewer's "24 pt renders 32 px" and it hits every screen: titles, tab labels, row text, counts, prompts, toasts (gameplay "Logging +1" sits on the toast's bottom edge), gathering "Uncommon timber at 10.0" runs 35 px past its card. Fix list 1.
- **B. Headings are bold; every concept title is regular weight** (Build Catalogue, Inventory, Gathering Skills, Market Board, Direct Trade, Sawmill, tracker titles). `Heading()` (`SFHudStyle.h:261-264`) forces `bBold = true`. Fix list 2.
- **C. Numerals are Source Sans Bold; every concept numeral is the EB Garamond text face, regular** (vitals "424 / 424", target "320 / 320", hotbar/bag/offer counts, market Qty/price, tracker "0/3", XP "420 / 1,000"). `Num()` (`SFHudStyle.h:253-258`) swaps to `NumeralFamily()` bold. menus.md row "Text" says Source Sans; the concepts do not. Fix list 2 (and the table).
- **D. Selected moss is the wrong green.** `tab_selected.png` centre is `#3E5543`; canonical moss is `#2B3B2C`, concept samples `#2A3B34` (build tab), `#28322D` (market row), `#1E251A` (trade Offer Locked). dE 8.9-12.4 on build tabs/tiles, market tabs/rail/row, trade lock button. One art fix covers `TabBrush`, `RowBrush`, `ButtonBrush(Pressed)`. Fix list 3.
- **E. - **F. Level badge.** `hex_level_badge.png` (232x232, 116 px) has no lower lobe; concepts are 115x158 with the numeral in a plate under the hexagon (gameplay, build, inventory, gathering, craft). Live draws "1" over the stag's chest (`SFE1VitalsHud.cpp:78-84`). Fix list 5.
