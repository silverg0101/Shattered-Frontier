# Female player start outfit: review, round 3 (final)

Concepts: `docs/evidence/player-outfit/concept-female/female_starter_turnaround.png`, `female_starter_closeup.png`
Live pairs: `r3-front-compare.png`, `r3-three_quarter-compare.png`, `r3-side-compare.png` (idle), `r3-walk_back-compare.png` (walking)
Previous open list: `REVIEW-round2.md`.
Out of scope: face, hair, lighting, background, hunched idle pose. Garment and body fit only.

## r3-front — FAIL

- Still an A-line: the cloth tapers in under the bust, then flares out to a hem wider than the hips (about shoulder width). It reads as a short dress. The concept is a straight tunic with near-vertical sides.
- The bust is still shaped, with a defined underbust shadow. The concept hangs loose with no waist.
- The neck slit is closed: no black void (fixed). The neck opening is still wide and scooped, and a skin-coloured lump of collarbone/trapezius shows proud of the neckline on the wearer's right. Body poke-through or a gap at the edge of the opening.
- The cloth now has a fine horizontal rib texture. Better than felt, but it reads as knit jersey rather than the concept's slubby, crumpled linen with crossing threads.
- Sleeve ends show a slightly thicker edge but no clear folded cuff band.
- Leggings are smooth and darker than the concept's mid-brown heathered knit.

## r3-three_quarter — FAIL

- Same flare from the underbust, same bust shaping and same collarbone lump at the neckline.
- The cuff on the near sleeve reads as a faint band (improved). The side slit isn't visible from this angle.

## r3-side — PASS (with notes)

- Straight tunic to mid-thigh with a readable side slit and the legging behind it. Recognisable as the concept.
- Notes: the hem swoops longer at the back (the concept's hem is level). No black line on the slit edge any more. The bust is slightly more shaped than in the concept.

## r3-walk_back — PASS (with notes)

- The back is one uninterrupted panel through the stride; the centre-back split is gone. It hangs mostly straight, with only a mild waist and seat taper.
- Notes: the right silhouette edge is still serrated/dithered. Small dark specks show at the right sleeve/side gap (the hand or body showing through). Minor dark artefacts at the lower-left slit corner.

## Round 2 items

1. Flared A-line skirt: open (unchanged from the front and three-quarter).

3. Centre-back split while walking: fixed.
4. Black void at the slit base: fixed (the slit is closed). The neck opening is still wide, with a new collarbone poke at the edge.
5. Linen weave: improved (texture added), but it reads as knit rib, not linen.
6. Leggings read as smooth tights: open.
7. No sleeve cuff: improved (faint band in three-quarter, unclear in front).
8. Black line on the slit edge: fixed.

## Fix list (for the owner to decide on; review cap reached)

1. A-line flare reads as a dress (front, three-quarter). ART: straighten the side seams from armpit to hem. Hem width = hip width + 4-6 cm ease, and remove the underbust intake. Engineer
2. Collarbone/skin pokes through at the neckline and the neck opening is wide (front, three-quarter). ART: pull the neckline in to the base of the throat and hide or shrink the body under it (or push the collar verts out past the clavicle). Engineer
3. Bust shaping still strong (front, three-quarter, side). ART: let the cloth bridge flat from the bust apex down; soften the bust normal and shading. Engineer
4. Cloth reads as knit rib (all views). ART: replace the horizontal rib normal with a linen plain-weave normal plus slub/crumple variation and irregular crossing threads. Codex
5. Leggings flat and too dark (all views). ART: add a heathered brown wool knit albedo/normal and lift the value to the concept's mid-brown. Codex
6. Back hem dips longer than the front (side). ART: level the hem. Engineer
7. No clear folded cuff (front). ART: model a 4 cm turned-back band at the sleeve end. Codex
8. Serrated right silhouette edge and dark specks at the sleeve gap (walk back). ART/CODE: check the masked/dithered opacity on the tunic material, and close the sleeve-to-side gap. Engineer
